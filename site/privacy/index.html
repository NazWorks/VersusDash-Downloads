Privacy Policy

Effective October 4, 2026

The short version

VersusDash does not ask for a name, email address, phone number, birth date, precise location, contacts, photos, or advertising identifier. There are no ads or data sales. Hosted play uses random identifiers so rooms, reconnects, and competitive records can work without a player profile.

Information used by hosted play

When you use hosted rooms, VersusDash processes random installation, session, room, match, and result identifiers; room state and selected level; assigned service region; gameplay version; scores, attempts, collectibles, and finish time; and basic gateway capacity and reliability events. Public leaderboards show a generic racer label, score, attempts, and time. They do not expose the underlying installation identifier.

Our hosting providers necessarily process network information such as an IP address to route requests, prevent abuse, and operate the service. NazWorks does not use that information for advertising or to build personal profiles.

Local information

The game stores settings, an opaque installation identifier, cosmetic ownership, and local progress on the device. Removing the app may remove local information depending on the platform and backup settings.

Why we use information

  • create, join, reconnect to, and clean up hosted rooms;
  • run multiplayer races and keep compatible clients together;
  • verify and rank completed runs;
  • diagnose availability, capacity, crashes, and abuse; and
  • respond when someone voluntarily emails support or feedback.

Retention and providers

Empty hosted rooms expire automatically. Operational room records and competitive results may remain while the alpha service operates so reconnects, reliability work, and leaderboards remain useful. Service logs are kept according to our hosting providers’ operational retention. The website and downloads use GitHub; hosted gameplay and records use Fly.io; Apple may process App Store and TestFlight activity under Apple’s own policies.

Children and families

VersusDash is designed for family play and does not provide chat, targeted advertising, public profiles, or a way to submit a child’s name. A parent or guardian should contact us for any privacy request involving a child.

Your choices

You can play locally without creating a hosted room. To ask about access or deletion of hosted records, email feedback@naz.works. We may provide steps for retrieving the app’s opaque installation identifier so we can locate the correct records without asking for a real-world identity.

Changes and contact

We will update this page when the game’s data practices change and revise the effective date above. Questions and requests can be sent to feedback@naz.works.
